Privacypolicy

Effective November 11, 2026. This is the privacy policy for the Big Gym Guy iPhone app.

Home Big Gym Guy Privacy Terms Support

Big Gym Guy is a free iPhone workout log and program planner from Sparks Endeavors. This version has no user accounts, no sign-in, no cloud backend, no ads, no third-party analytics, and no in-app purchases. Workout history and journal notes stay on your iPhone unless you export them. We do not sell your information.

This policy describes the Big Gym Guy app. It does not describe other pages on sparksendeavors.com.

Who we are

Big Gym Guy is published by Sparks Endeavors. For privacy questions, email josh@sparksendeavors.com.

Not a medical device

Big Gym Guy is a workout log. It is not a medical device and it is not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. The terms of use say the same thing.

What stays on your iPhone

The app stores your workout history and journal notes on the device. That includes the programs, workouts, sets, weights, reps, RPE, and notes you enter, along with the training calendar and exercise charts built from that history.

Sparks Endeavors does not receive that log. There is no Big Gym Guy account and no Sparks Endeavors server that collects it. A backup you make through Apple, such as an iPhone backup, is Apple's feature. It is not a Big Gym Guy account, and we cannot see it.

Export and import

You can export your data to a file, and you can import a program or data file you choose. An export is a copy you control. That file can leave your iPhone if you send it or save it somewhere else — for example by email, AirDrop, or the Files app. Big Gym Guy does not upload your workouts to a Sparks Endeavors server.

After a file leaves the device, that copy is outside the app. Deleting Big Gym Guy does not delete files you already exported.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional and write-only. If you allow it, Big Gym Guy can save a finished workout to Apple Health. The saved item is a traditional strength-training workout with a start time, an end time, and an estimate of active energy.

The app does not read Apple Health. It does not read your workouts, heart rate, or any other Health data. Health data is not used for advertising and is not sold. You can refuse the permission when asked, and you can change it later in iOS Settings.

Notifications

Notifications are only for the rest timer. The app uses them to tell you when a rest period ends. It does not send marketing notifications. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings.

YouTube search

The exercise library can open a YouTube search for a lift in Safari or the YouTube app, so you can look up a form video. Big Gym Guy does not send your workout history, journal, or other data stored in the app to YouTube. Once you leave the app, YouTube and your browser follow their own policies.

Ads, analytics, and purchases

This version of Big Gym Guy does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics, and does not include in-app purchases. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Children

Big Gym Guy is not directed at children under 13, and it is not a Made for Kids app. An App Store listing is public, so minors may still see it. There is no server-side collection of personal data. Workout history and notes the app keeps are stored on the device.

A parent who wants that on-device data removed can delete the app. Exported files, if any, have to be deleted wherever those copies were saved. Questions can go to josh@sparksendeavors.com.

Deleting the app

Deleting Big Gym Guy from your iPhone deletes the workout history and journal notes stored in the app. There is no account to close and no Sparks Endeavors cloud copy to erase. Copies you already exported remain until you delete those files yourself.

Email you send us

If you email josh@sparksendeavors.com for support, we use that message to reply and to help with the app. We do not sell those messages. Email is not the place for medical records or anything you would not want in an inbox.

Apple

Apple provides the iPhone, the App Store, Apple Health, notifications, and device backup. Apple's own privacy policy covers how Apple handles information in those systems. Sparks Endeavors does not operate those systems.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. This page is the privacy policy for Big Gym Guy: https://sparksendeavors.com/biggymguy/privacy/.
