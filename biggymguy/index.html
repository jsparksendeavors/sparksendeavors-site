Big GymGuy

A no-nonsense lifting log for people who just want to train. Free, from Sparks Endeavors.

Pick a program, open today's workout, and log your sets as you lift. A rest timer pops up when you check off a set. When you finish, you get a quick recap of what you trained. No account, no ads, and no in-app purchases in this version.

Follow a plan

Start with a built-in program, or build your own workouts and programs and set your training days. Short on time? Start a quick workout and log whatever you do.

  • Full Body Foundations
  • Upper / Lower Strength
  • Push Pull Legs
  • PHUL Hybrid
  • 5×5 Strength Base

Log fast at the gym

  • Weights, reps, and RPE per set
  • Adjustable rest timer with alerts
  • Plate math for loading the bar
  • Swap or add lifts mid-workout without losing finished sets
  • An exercise library with a one-tap YouTube search for form videos

See your progress

  • Full workout history and a training calendar
  • Charts for each exercise
  • A journal for notes on how training is going

Your data, your phone

  • No account and no sign-up
  • Workouts are stored on your iPhone
  • Back up or move your data with export and import
  • Optionally save a finished workout to Apple Health. That connection is write-only: Big Gym Guy does not read your Health data
